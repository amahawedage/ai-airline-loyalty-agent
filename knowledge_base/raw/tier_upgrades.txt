AIRLINE LOYALTY PROGRAMME – TIER UPGRADE POLICY

Topic: Tier Upgrades

Tier qualification depends on the member's current loyalty status and qualifying tier miles.

The assistant should retrieve the member's current membership tier and tier-miles balance before providing tier-upgrade guidance.

The following prototype qualification thresholds are used:

Silver tier: 25,000 tier miles
Gold tier: 50,000 tier miles

A member with sufficient qualifying tier miles may be eligible for the corresponding tier.

The assistant must distinguish between redeemable miles and tier miles. Redeemable miles are used for rewards, while tier miles are used for tier qualification.

The assistant must not claim that a member has qualified for a tier without checking the member's available tier miles.

If the available information is insufficient to determine eligibility, the assistant should explain the limitation and request the required information or escalate the case.

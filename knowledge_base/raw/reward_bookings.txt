AIRLINE LOYALTY PROGRAMME – REWARD BOOKING POLICY

Topic: Reward Bookings

Reward bookings require sufficient redeemable miles and availability of the requested reward.

The assistant should identify the requested origin, destination, cabin and available member miles when evaluating a reward booking.

The prototype reward database contains reward options with a required mileage amount and an availability status.

A reward is eligible when:
1. The member has sufficient redeemable miles.
2. The requested reward is available.

If the member does not have enough miles, the assistant should calculate or explain the mileage shortfall.

If the requested reward is unavailable, the assistant should clearly state that availability is currently unavailable.

The assistant must not claim that a reward booking has been completed. The prototype provides eligibility and guidance only.

If the requested reward cannot be evaluated using the available information, the assistant should request the missing information or escalate the case.

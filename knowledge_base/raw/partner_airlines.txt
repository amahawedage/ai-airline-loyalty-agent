AIRLINE LOYALTY PROGRAMME – PARTNER AIRLINE POLICY

Topic: Partner Airlines

Partner-airline eligibility depends on the partner airline and applicable earning or redemption rules.

The prototype partner-airline database contains:
- Partner ID
- Partner Name
- Eligibility
- Earning Rule
- Redemption Rule

Partner Air:
Eligibility: Eligible on qualifying flights
Earning rule: Based on eligible fare
Redemption rule: Subject to reward availability

Global Connect:
Eligibility: Eligible for selected fares
Earning rule: Fare-class dependent
Redemption rule: Limited redemption

The assistant must retrieve the applicable partner-airline information before answering partner questions.

The assistant should not assume that all flights or fares qualify.

If the required partner information is unavailable, the assistant should explain the limitation and escalate if necessary.

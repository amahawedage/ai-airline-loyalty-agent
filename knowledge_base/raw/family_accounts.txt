AIRLINE LOYALTY PROGRAMME – FAMILY ACCOUNT POLICY

Topic: Family Accounts

A family account can contain multiple members associated with the same family account identifier.

The assistant should retrieve the family account associated with the member before answering family-account questions.

The prototype family account data contains:
- Family Account ID
- Member ID
- Relationship
- Account Status

Only information available in the synthetic database should be presented as member-specific information.

The assistant must not invent family members or relationships.

If the member does not have a family account, the assistant should clearly state that no family account information is available for that member.

If the requested family-account information cannot be retrieved, the assistant should explain the limitation and escalate where appropriate.

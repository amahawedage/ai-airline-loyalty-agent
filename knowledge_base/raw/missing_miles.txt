AIRLINE LOYALTY PROGRAMME – MISSING MILES POLICY

Topic: Missing Miles

Members may report missing miles when an eligible flight or transaction has not been credited to their loyalty account.

To investigate missing miles, the assistant should identify the member and, where available, the relevant transaction. The transaction status should be checked before providing guidance.

A transaction with status Credited has already been credited and should not be treated as missing.

A transaction with status Missing indicates that the miles have not been credited.

A transaction with status Pending indicates that the transaction is still being processed and may require additional processing time.

The assistant must not invent missing transaction information. If the required member or transaction information cannot be found, the assistant should explain what information is required.

If the case cannot be resolved using the available information, the case should be escalated to human customer support.

For this prototype, member and transaction information is retrieved from the synthetic SQLite loyalty database.
